Practitioner Take

The rapid exploitation lifecycle, coupled with the increasing autonomy and resource acquisition capabilities of AI agents, presents a significant shift. Traditional perimeter security is insufficient against agentic threats that can bypass defenses by acquiring credentials or exploiting zero-days. Prompt injection remains a persistent threat, even in encrypted inference scenarios, necessitating deeper architectural controls. The focus must move towards robust runtime authorization and provenance tracking for AI-acquired resources, alongside intrinsic model-level defenses against prompt manipulation.
